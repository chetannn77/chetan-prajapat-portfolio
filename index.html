01 / ABOUT ME

Grounded in Real Work. Driven by Curiosity.

I come from Prajapati Nagar village in Bichhawadi (Sanchore tehsil, Jalore district, Rajasthan, India). After completing my Class 10 education in 2021 at Government Senior Secondary School, Bichhawadi, I focused directly on developing practical, real-world skills to support my family and build a strong professional foundation.

Through hands-on dedication on active job sites, I grew into a stainless steel fabrication contractor. My work involves building custom railings, main gates, balcony railings, and glass railing installations—alongside taking full responsibility for site measurements, interpreting technical drawings, preparing accurate quotations, and coordinating project delivery. My practical journey also includes working on a project at Chennai Airport.

Alongside physical site work, I taught myself how to use computers and Microsoft Excel to organize calculations and manage estimates more effectively. That practical need sparked a deeper curiosity in technology—leading me to study foundational HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and explore how web development, AI tools, and automation can modernize everyday business operations.

05 / MY DIGITAL LEARNING

From Site Measurements to Digital Tools

How Practical Work Sparked My Tech Journey

My introduction to technology did not begin in a computer science classroom—it began with the practical need to manage measurements, material lists, and customer quotations accurately. Starting on my own, I learned how to operate a computer and use Microsoft Excel to organize numbers, reduce manual calculation mistakes, and prepare clearer documentation.

Seeing how much time a well-structured spreadsheet could save made me curious about how websites and software work under the hood. I began learning the building blocks of the web—HTML5, CSS3, and foundational JavaScript—step by step through self-study.

Today, I am actively exploring how web development, AI tools, and workflow automation can help contractors, small businesses, and everyday professionals reduce repetitive work, keep records organized, and communicate more clearly.
